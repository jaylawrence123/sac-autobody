What we do

Services

  • Hail Damage

    Paintless dent removal and conventional repair. We handle everything from a few dings to a full roof — and work directly with your insurance.

  • Collision Repair

    Fender benders to major impacts — deer strikes, rollovers, and structural damage. Restored to pre-accident condition, every time.

  • Paint Refinishing

    Factory-matched color, manufacturer-spec prep, and full clear coat application. Correction and enhancement included — no shortcuts.

  • Auto Glass

    Windshield, back glass, and door glass on all makes. OEM and OEM-equivalent glass. ADAS recalibration available after replacement.

  • Rust Repair

    Surface oxidation to full panel rot. We cut it out, weld in new metal, and refinish. No filler over rust — ever.

  • Frame Straightening

    Structural frame and unibody pulled to manufacturer spec on computer-measured equipment. Verified before any panel work starts.

  • RV Paint & Body

    Full paint and body work for motorhomes, travel trailers, and specialty vehicles. Gel coat, fiberglass, aluminum — same standard as any passenger vehicle.

Meet Sean.

Sean Reagan started SAC Auto Body in 2017 with the support of his wife Shannon, after more than a decade working at other reputable repair facilities in Rapid City. The shop runs on one principle: earn the customer’s trust before starting the repair.

Sean and his crew work on every vehicle that comes through the shop. Sean returns your calls personally and walks you through every step. And yes — Pickles, the shop dog, will greet you at the door.
